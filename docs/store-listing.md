## Description (4000)

Rush is a friendly health assistant made for Ghana. Describe how you feel and get clear, plain-English guidance on what to do next: rest at home, talk to a pharmacist, visit a clinic or CHPS compound, or get emergency care.

**Chat with Rush**
Ask health questions in your own words, any time. Rush explains what your symptoms commonly mean and where to go, and if anything sounds like an emergency it shows you the 112 and ambulance (193) numbers straight away.

**Symptoms Checker**
Answer a few simple questions and get a clear next step. Every fever comes with a reminder to get a malaria test, and young children, pregnant women and older people are guided to care sooner.

**Track your health**
Log blood pressure, blood sugar, weight, temperature, mood and symptoms. See simple charts and a plain-language note on every reading.

**Never miss a dose**
Add your medicines and get reminders at the right times, even without internet. Mark each dose taken or skipped.

**Emergency SOS**
One tap to call 112, the National Ambulance Service (193), police or fire, plus your emergency contact and a medical ID with your blood group, allergies and conditions.

**Health Library and Recipes**
Short, trusted articles on malaria, blood pressure, diabetes, sickle cell, pregnancy, children's danger signs, mental health and first aid, plus lighter versions of familiar Ghanaian dishes. All work offline.

**Private by design**
No account and no ads. Your health records stay on your phone. You can export a summary for your doctor or delete everything at any time.

Rush gives general health information and is not a substitute for a doctor. In an emergency, call 112.

## App privacy answers

**Apple App Privacy** ("nutrition label"):
- Data used to track you: **None**.
- Data linked to you: **None**. No accounts, so nothing is linked to an identity.
- Data not linked to you: **Health & Fitness** and **Other User Content** (chat messages), collected only when the user sends a chat. Purpose: **App Functionality**. Not stored by the developer.

**Google Play Data safety**:
- Data collected: **Health info** and **Messages (other in-app messages)**, optional, processed ephemerally for app functionality, not shared for advertising, encrypted in transit.
- Data shared: sent to a service provider (Anthropic) to generate replies.
- Users can request deletion: yes (all data is on the device; Settings → Delete all my data).
